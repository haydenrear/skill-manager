chm-home-sync-tlc.txt -- three-tier home reconciliation (home sync / home close-out)
Recorded 2026-07-27. tlc2 2.19. Every run under the 120s tlc_seconds budget.

==================== DECOMPOSITION, AND WHY ====================
The sync slice was first authored as ONE spec at full width: three home tiers,
two paths, and a per-write log carrying the destination home and three
booleans. Measured:

  three tiers x two paths, full write log
    Progress(11)  2,393,751 generated / 312,704 distinct / 132,161 queued
    Progress(12)  4,574,065 generated / 574,209 distinct / 223,650 queued
    -> still climbing at 120s, 11x over budgets.max_distinct_states 50000.

The timeout was NOT raised. Two changes, each of which removes states that no
invariant relates:

  1. The write log became <<path, reason>> pairs instead of one tuple per write
     carrying the destination and three booleans. Same information for every
     invariant that reads it; the destination component was never read.
       -> Progress(17) 3,884,494 generated / 456,874 distinct. Not enough.

  2. The slice was split along its two independent axes, as CONSTANTS so a
     config picks one and pins the other:
       SyncTiers  {root, project, worktree} | {project, worktree}
       SyncPaths  {p1}                      | {p1, p2}
     Three tiers is what the provenance / merge-base / close-out properties
     need: each turns on a destination whose content came from a home that is
     not the one now pushing, which two homes cannot express. Two paths is what
     the merge algebra needs: the difference between "each side moved a
     different file" and "both moved the same one", and the only way a
     reconciliation can write SOME of a unit. No invariant relates a tier
     question to a path question, so checking them together was multiplying two
     state spaces to check nothing new.
       -> 180 and 283 distinct states. 1s each.
